# QuantumPay Shield Video Voiceover

## Recommended format

- Duration: about 35 to 40 seconds
- Aspect ratio: 16:9
- Style: project demo plus academic explainer
- Speed: medium, with enough pause to explain each step

## Scene script

1. Opening
   "This is QuantumPay Shield, a secure online payment prototype that uses quantum cryptography to protect digital transactions."

2. Payment entry
   "Alice enters Bob's payment details in the Streamlit app and starts the transaction."

3. Password verification
   "Before the quantum process begins, the system verifies the sender using the UPI password."

4. Secure background process
   "In the background, Alice and Bob perform BB84 quantum key distribution. Random qubits are exchanged, bases are compared, and QBER is calculated."

5. Successful payment
   "When QBER remains below the threshold, the channel is secure, the shared key is accepted, the payload is encrypted, and the payment is approved."

6. Failed payment
   "If an eavesdropper intercepts the qubits, their quantum state changes, QBER rises above the threshold, and the payment is blocked immediately."

7. Closing
   "This project demonstrates both secure payment authorization and attack detection, making the transaction flow easier to understand and explain."

## Presentation tip

Play this video before the live demo, then open the Streamlit app and repeat the same success path and failure path in real time.
